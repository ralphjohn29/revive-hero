Summary

Twelve treatments of the “What professionals say.” section: 01–06 on light ground, 07–12 on brand navy. 05 and 11 carry a Book a Tour primary with the unified darken hover. 06, 10 and 12 are interactive — click around.

Quotes are samples. They get replaced with her 7 real Google reviews before launch.

Divi compatibility

  • Each option ships as one Code-module block — CSS + markup + a few lines of vanilla JS, no external libraries
  • Strip options (06, 12) swipe natively on mobile with zero JS
  • Primary buttons follow the unified darken hover (gold → deep gold)
