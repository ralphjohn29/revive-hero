Six Ways to Show the Four Reasons

Six designs for the “Why choose Revive?” cards, all using your four headings and descriptions, with a matching gold icon for each heading. None of them need instructions on the card: an icon, a photo or the layout shows people there’s more to see.

Try them: on the desktop previews, move your mouse over a card. On the phone previews, tap a card, just like on a real phone. No cartoon pictures, only real Revive photos and the Revive colors.

The Corner Icon

A clean white card with an icon, the heading and a short line, plus a small gold + in the corner. The + tells people there’s more without any words. It flips on hover on desktop and on tap on phones, and the + turns into × on the back.
