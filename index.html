Summary

01 The Signature Welcome converts fastest — book a tour right in the hero, zero scroll to act. 02 The Confident Rate pre-qualifies with price and is the lightest build. 03 The Professional Showcase sells the community, strongest for directory-led growth.

Every variant keeps Book a Tour and a tap-to-call number in the nav on desktop and mobile — two ways to reach Julia’s team, always one tap away. Same locked copy, same palette, all three under the 500KB above-the-fold budget. Pick one — or blend — and we refine from there.

Locked copy — identical in all three

  • H1: Private Salon Suites for Rent in Raleigh, NC (production page’s single H1)
  • Sub: License a private, fully-equipped suite and keep 100% of your profits. Move-in ready, all utilities included.
  • Primary CTA: Book a Tour — gold, always
  • Scarcity: Now Leasing: Limited Signature Suites
  • Trust: 5.0 stars on Google
  • Vocabulary: “license a suite”, never “rent” (H1 keyword excepted — it’s the search term)

Production notes

  • (919) 346-6607 is a placeholder — swap in Revive’s real number; keep the tel: link so one tap dials
  • Price figure removed per client preference — Option 02 carries the all-inclusive promise, no public rate; revisit only if she changes her mind
  • “28 professionals” and directory avatars in Variant 3 are illustrative — swap for real tenant count
  • All photography = direction only. Final art: warm, golden, in-use suite shots from the Revive photo shoot; then rewrite alt text to match
  • Fonts: DM Serif Display (headlines) + Josefin Sans (body/UI) — the only pair; subset to used weights
  • Palette locked: charcoal #1C1B1A, off-white #FAF7F2, gold #C6A55C (actions/highlights only), taupe #E7DFD3. Two CTA colors max (gold primary, charcoal secondary) — respected in all three
  • Accessibility: pinch-zoom allowed, one H1, descriptive alt, explicit image dimensions, 44px+ touch targets, gold paired with charcoal text for contrast
  • Banned & absent: popups, hero carousels, white-spa stock, glow effects
