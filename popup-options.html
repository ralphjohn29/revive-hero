Seven Designs for the Mailing List Pop-up

Seven ways to show the mailing list pop-up. Each one is a compact card over a darkened backdrop, so the page still shows behind it and visitors can easily close it. On phones it stays centered on the screen with space around it, and the close button is always easy to see.

Every design keeps the same form fields (first name, last name, email, phone) and the same SMS consent wording, word for word. Timing stays as agreed: after 30 seconds, or when someone is about to leave.
