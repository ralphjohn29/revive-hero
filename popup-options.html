Four Designs for the Mailing List Pop-up

Four ways to show the mailing list pop-up. Each one is a compact card over a darkened backdrop, so the page still shows behind it and visitors can easily close it. On phones it fits the screen with room to spare, or slides up from the bottom.

Every design keeps the same form fields (first name, last name, email, phone) and the same SMS consent wording, word for word. Timing stays as agreed: after 30 seconds, or when someone is about to leave.

The Two-Step

Step 1 asks only for a name and email. Step 2 asks for the phone number and SMS consent. A shorter first step usually means more people sign up. It’s the smallest card at 420px, and a bottom sheet on phones. Use the buttons to switch steps.
