Six carousel treatments for the “Now Open — Limited Suites Remaining” section, all running the real suite photos from her current carousel. 01 and 04 are swipe-first strips (native mobile swiping). 02 and 05 sit on the navy ground. 03 is the classic one-at-a-time fade with a counter. 06 adds a thumbnail rail.

Divi compatibility

  • Each option ships as one copy-paste block for a Divi Code module — CSS + markup + a few lines of vanilla JS, no external libraries, no iframes
  • Scroll-snap options (01, 02, 04) even work with zero JavaScript on mobile — native swipe
  • Replaces the current third-party carousel on the live A Look Inside page
  • Photos are the real suite shots from her media library — swap for Revive shoot photos at launch
