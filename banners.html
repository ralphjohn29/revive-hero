Summary

01 The Crest Banner is her current banner rebuilt in the new system. 02 The Gold Gradient and 03 The Mirage Fade swap the ground color. 04 The Photo Dim adds a real suite photo. 05 The Slim Crest Bar drops the headline so the hero below carries it. 06 The Split Stage is the compact cousin of the approved split hero.

Placement options: as a brand banner above the split hero, or as a compact replacement hero if she wants the shorter page. Either way the H1 stays on the page exactly once for SEO.

Notes

  • All six use her official crest and wordmark files, gold #DB9446, navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B
  • Sample CTA labels — live site says “Partner With Us”; our build uses “Book a Tour” or keeps hers, her call
  • If used as a compact hero, the split hero is retired — the two are alternatives, not stackables (except 05, designed to stack)
  • H1 rule: exactly one H1 per page — whichever banner carries it, the split hero drops it
