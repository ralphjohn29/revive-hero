Summary

One slim header band, reused on every inner page — only the eyebrow, H1, and intro change. It carries the page H1 (each page needs exactly one) and sits directly under the global nav. No hero imagery, no tall band — content starts immediately after.

Ready-made H1s for the first three pages: How to Join → “How to Join a Salon Suite in Raleigh, NC” · A Look Inside → “Explore Our Luxury Salon Suites in Raleigh, NC” · Contact → “Contact Revive Salon Collective”.

Notes

  • Exactly one H1 per page, inside the band — fixes the current pages that are missing H1s or doubling them
  • Eyebrow, H1, and intro swap per page; the band itself is identical site-wide
  • All six use the official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B, EB Garamond (Arno Pro stand-in)
  • Option 01 is the pattern already used in the How to Join v2 draft — picking another option restyles all inner-page headers in one pass
