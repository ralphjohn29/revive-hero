Revive Salon Collective — Three Footer Directions

Three ways to close out the homepage, built to sit under the winning hero (The Signature Welcome). Same content — six different structures. Options 01–03 use the original preview palette. Options 04–06 are built from Julia’s official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), Blue Mirage #48516B (Pantone 2374C), her logo lockups, and EB Garamond as the web stand-in for Arno Pro.

Part of the same preview set: professionals section options · hero layout options · branding options for The Signature Welcome. Every footer repeats the one job of the page: get a beauty professional to book a suite tour.

Summary

01 The Classic Column is the traditional choice — everything visible, quiet confidence. 02 The Navy Standard matches her current site identity and the navy navs of the winning hero. 03 The Closer Band ends the page on the Book a Tour ask instead of the legal line.

Same content in all six: logo, tagline, links, contact, Book a Tour, legal. Pick one (or blend) and it becomes the build standard. 04–06 use the official brand kit from Julia’s files (gold #DB9446, Blue Mirage #48516B, official logo lockups).

Shared content — all three

  • Links: How to Join · A Look Inside · Directory · Contact + Privacy / Terms
  • Contact: (919) 346-6607 (her live number) · business Gmail · Raleigh, NC
  • CTA: Book a Tour — gold, repeated from the hero
  • Tagline: Elevate. Own. Inspire. / Your Signature Space Awaits.

Production notes

  • Mailing-list capture (full name + email) included in every option — replaces the old popup; real build = Fluent Forms feeding her existing mailing list
  • Street address intentionally left as “Raleigh, North Carolina” — add the full address once confirmed with Julia
  • Email shown is her business Gmail — confirm before launch whether a domain email should replace it
  • Legal links point to her existing /privacy/ and /terms/ pages
  • All three are pure CSS — zero JS, one shared logo image, under 80KB total
  • Pinch-zoom allowed, descriptive alt text, 44px+ touch targets, contrast-checked pairs
