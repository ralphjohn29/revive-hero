Summary

Six treatments of the “Joining is Simple” steps from her live How to Join page — same icons, same verbatim step copy, same Partner With Us close. 01 is the live layout in the official gold; 02–06 explore card, navy, flow-graphic, Mirage, and band structures.

Slots into the How to Join v2 page right after the slim header band, replacing the current three-step row.

Notes

  • Icons are the actual icons8 files from her live page — reused as-is on light grounds; for dark grounds they can sit in white chips (02) or gold outlines (03)
  • All step copy is verbatim from her live page
  • All six use the official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B, EB Garamond (Arno Pro stand-in)
  • Partner With Us label kept verbatim from her live page — swap to Book a Tour if preferred
