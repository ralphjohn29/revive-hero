Six Designs for the Cookie Notice

Six ways to let visitors know the website uses cookies, all in Revive’s navy, gold and cream with the same fonts as the site. Each is shown on a laptop and a phone. Click any button to see it close, then use Show again to bring it back.

Every design gives visitors a clear way to accept or decline, with both buttons equally easy to find, and links to the Privacy Policy. Once someone chooses, it won’t ask again.

01

The Navy Bar

A full-width navy strip along the bottom of the screen with a thin gold line on top. It’s the most familiar style, and it never covers the main content. On phones the buttons stack below the text.

02

The Corner Card

A small white card in the bottom-left corner with the Revive crest. It feels branded without taking over the page, and it sits on the opposite side from any chat or call buttons.

03

The Cream Ribbon

The quietest option: one slim cream line with a gold border, floating above the bottom of the page. Visitors can keep browsing and answer whenever they like.

04

The Welcome Card

A centered card that dims the page and asks before visitors start browsing. It’s the most noticeable choice and the strongest for privacy, but it’s one more step before someone sees the site.

05

The Preferences Panel

Lets visitors choose each type of cookie: necessary, analytics and marketing. It gives visitors the most control and is the style used by many larger brands. Try the switches.

06

The Suite Photo Card

A corner card with a Revive lobby photo on top, so even the cookie notice feels like part of the building. It matches the photo style used across the website.

What every design does

  • Accept and decline are equally easy to find.
  • Links to the Privacy Policy.
  • Once someone chooses, it won’t ask again on that device.

On phones

  • Buttons are full-size and easy to tap.
  • Nothing runs off the edge of the screen.
  • It sits apart from the mailing list pop-up, so the two never overlap.
