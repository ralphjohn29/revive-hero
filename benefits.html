Summary

The eight benefits from her live How to Join page, copy kept verbatim with two corrections (110–180, Wi-Fi). 01 The Ledger is the direct upgrade of her current layout. 02–05 explore checklist, navy, gold-top, and Mirage treatments. 06 The Split Frame turns the section title into its own framed panel.

Slots into the How to Join v2 page right after the steps section.

Notes

  • Copy is her live page verbatim except the two corrections: “110–180 Sq. Ft.” and “Wi-Fi” (live page reads “110 180” and “Wi Fi”)
  • All six use the official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B, EB Garamond (Arno Pro stand-in)
  • Pairing: 01 pairs with the Gold Ledger steps (04 on joining-steps), 03 pairs with the Navy options
