Summary

Six photo-background header bands for every inner page — the global nav sits on top, the page H1 lives inside the band, and content starts right after. Same H1 sample on all six; eyebrow and intro swap per page.

01, 02, and 04 use her real storefront photo; 02 uses a craft photo; 03 and 05 use craft/aesthetician imagery. All are stand-ins — final photos come from the Revive shoot.

Notes

  • All six use the official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B, EB Garamond (Arno Pro stand-in)
  • Exactly one H1 per page, carried inside the band
  • Scrims and gradients keep text at AA contrast over any photo — swap photos freely without breaking readability
  • Option 06 needs no scrim — text never touches the photo
