Summary

One slim header band reused on every inner page — the global nav stays, the band carries the page H1 (exactly one per page), and content starts immediately after. Eyebrow, H1, and intro swap per page; the band itself is identical site-wide.

Ready-made H1s for the first three pages: How to Join → “How to Join a Salon Suite in Raleigh, NC” · A Look Inside → “Explore Our Luxury Salon Suites in Raleigh, NC” · Contact → “Contact Revive Salon Collective”.

Notes

  • The nav shown is the approved homepage nav (crest, links, phone, gold Book a Tour)
  • 02 makes the nav and band read as one continuous navy block; 01, 03, and 06 keep a hard light band under the navy nav
  • All six use the official brand kit: gold #DB9446 (Pantone 722C), navy #121E3F, Blue Mirage #48516B, EB Garamond (Arno Pro stand-in)
  • Picking one applies to How to Join, A Look Inside, Contact, and any future inner page in a single pass
