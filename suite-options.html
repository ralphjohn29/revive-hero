A New “Suite Options” Section

The FAQ says our studios come in different sizes, but the homepage doesn’t show that yet. This section would let visitors see the suite types before they book a tour. It sits right after the “Your Own Studio. Your Own Success.” gallery, so there’s another visual section before the page moves into mostly text.

Everything here is a sample. Suite names, sizes and which photo goes with which suite are placeholders using the current gallery photos. They’re there to show the layout. Julia would confirm the real suite types, sizes and photos (the October 23 photo shoot could be a good source). No prices are shown.

Why after the gallery

  • Visitors have just seen the space. Next they ask, “which one fits me?”

  • Every button leads to the same private tour form

What stays the same

  • No prices, as with the FAQ
  • Same fonts, colors and spacing as the rest of the homepage
  • Nothing goes on the website until it’s approved
